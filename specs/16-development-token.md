# 16 — Token launch on Robinhood Chain

**Planned milestone: TOKEN LAUNCH ON ROBINHOOD CHAIN.**

Status: roadmap / design only. No deployment, sale, token address or allocation
has been approved or implemented. Game coins are not blockchain tokens.

## Objective

Evaluate whether a transferable token adds sustainable utility and funds useful
project work better than subscriptions, grants, sponsorship or voluntary support.
The free paper demo must remain usable without a token or a deposit.

## Sequence and gates

1. **Product evidence:** reliable paper alpha, repeat use and measured operating
   costs. Proposal target: at least 100 weekly active users with four consecutive
   measured weeks; this is a decision gate, not a forecast or achievement.
2. **Utility and funding memo:** explain what holding/spending enables, who pays
   for infrastructure, and why an ordinary credit/subscription is insufficient.
   Candidate utility: optional tournament creation or community feature voting;
   no guaranteed profit, yield, price support or ownership claim.
3. **Legal review:** determine issuer, launch locations, user eligibility,
   marketing/disclosure and tax obligations with qualified counsel. Jurisdiction
   and token design are currently unknown; no compliance conclusion is made.
4. **Economics proposal:** total/max supply, mint/burn/admin powers, distribution,
   treasury budget, community allocation, team vesting/cliff, circulating-supply
   schedule and downside cases. Do not invent percentages before a cost model.
5. **Implementation:** target Robinhood Chain; verify network support, actual costs
   and deployment tooling before implementation;
   public contract source, tests, testnet distribution, vesting, multisig treasury,
   independent audit and documented emergency/admin powers.
6. **Go/no-go:** resolve audit findings and required disclosures; approve a final
   launch plan separately. Publish treasury addresses, expenditure reports and
   vesting transparency if launched. Never use an unverified local leaderboard
   to distribute financially valuable rewards.

## Acceptance

A dated decision record covers alternatives, operating runway, risks, approvals,
contract verification and ownership of each admin role. A roadmap entry alone
must never trigger issuance or become a promise of returns.
